Academy Policies

Terms of Service

Code of conduct, fair play policies, and subscription terms for members of The Last Pawn Chess Academy.

1. Fair Play & Anti-Cheating

All members agree to uphold the highest standards of chess sportsmanship. The use of chess engines, bots, database assistance during live games, or external assistance during official Academy tournaments and challenges is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate account revocation.

2. Academy Membership & Coaching

Coaching sessions, course materials, and downloadable PGN study guides are licensed solely for the personal educational use of the registered member and may not be reproduced, redistributed, or broadcast without prior written consent.

3. Tournament Regulations

All Academy-sanctioned events follow standard FIDE Laws of Chess. Tournament directors reserve the right to adjudicate pairings, time controls, and dispute resolutions impartially.
